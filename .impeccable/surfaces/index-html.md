# Character studio

Scope: the active standalone ClipLab studio for authoring product companions, expression beats, looping sequences, and app or marketing exports.

The user-specified direction is dark and monochrome: a transparent header above three separate floating charcoal cards for preview, sequence, and inspector. Controls, selected states, and timeline beats remain gray; active beats use a lighter charcoal fill and clear border. Character colors provide the color. The supplied white ClipLab SVG is binding, reduced to 60% of its previous size. Use restrained typography, soft card corners, and generous separation between the three main surfaces.

Compact Left, Front, Right, Back, and reset controls replace the orientation globe. Dragging and keyboard rotation remain available. Actual CSS-size presets, Fit, True front, position lock, eye tracking, and body tracking support in-product review. Eye and body tracking are independent; True front takes precedence over body turns.

Animation creation reveals the selected animation editor. Add expression opens a visual picker, appends and previews the selected step, and confirms the change. Animations can be deleted and restored with Undo; one animation remains required. Import and Undo repair stale selections.

Set 1 keeps simple circular eyes and rounded mouths. Set 2 adds expressive brows, blush, half-lids, white eyes with pupils, kisses, and tongue-out faces. The libraries create editable expressions or replace the selected beat's face without replacing other saved expressions. Circular cheek cutouts, thicker mouth strokes, face elevation, and optional white highlights remain available. Candle light uses one crisp, rounded inset shadow treatment; props and tears ease through repeatable loops. Existing CSS-size detail rules and portable export behavior remain binding.
